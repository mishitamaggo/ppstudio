Frequently asked questions

Honest answers about how we partner, what projects cost, and how our studio model works.

We help founders build brands that people notice, fall in love with, and keep buying. Instead of handing you a standalone logo or running generic ads to a forgettable website, we bring brand positioning, tactile packaging design, storefront copywriting, and search/paid discovery together into one thoughtful project. When every touchpoint tells the same story, customers trust you instantly and your marketing works ten times harder.

Our studio engagements are structured as focused 4–5 week sprints starting at approximately ₹50,000 (INR). We don't believe in hourly billing or nickel-and-diming you for revisions. You get an all-in, dedicated creative partnership that covers positioning, visual identity, packaging dielines and print finishes, storefront storytelling, and your search/ad launch strategy · ready to go to market without bloated agency retainers.

Most traditional agencies sell fragmented menus: one person makes a logo, another boosts social posts, and an intern writes captions. Freelancers can be talented, but you get stuck playing project manager between four people who never talk to each other. Parcel Play works as a single, senior creative partner. We ensure that the paper weight and unboxing experience of your physical product matches the tone of your Meta video ads and the exact search keywords on your product pages.

We partner closely with emerging consumer product brands and ambitious D2C founders · especially in skincare, specialty food & beverage, jewellery, lifestyle goods, and functional wellness. We also work with established small-to-midsize businesses that have outgrown their original branding and need to reposition for higher-margin customers.

Across partner brand campaigns, our work has achieved an average Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) of 4.6x (compared to the 2.5x e-commerce industry baseline), generated over 11,000 top-3 keyword rankings on Google Search, scaled monthly organic revenues from $120k to $360k, and built 37 custom brand automation systems. We believe good design that doesn't move the business forward is just expensive decoration.
