The 5 Questions Framework

Five simple, honest questions we ask before writing a word or drawing a line · in this exact order.

01

Who is it for, really?

Not broad demographics like "men aged 25–40". We study what people actually search for at midnight, what questions linger in their head, and what anxiety stops them from buying.

02

What are they actually buying?

Nobody buys coffee just for beans, or skincare just for chemicals. They buy a morning ritual, peace of mind, or self-assurance. We find the real emotional job your product does.

03

How do we break the category script?

We look at what everyone else in your industry is saying · usually the same glossy clichés · and deliberately choose a clearer, more honest way to talk.

04

What is the central idea?

One unmistakable campaign thesis and creative direction that guides your packaging, your website copy, your photography, and your team for the next year.

05

Did it actually move the needle?

Good design shouldn't hide behind art school excuses. We track the numbers: did ad spend return at 4x+? Did organic search traffic climb? Did customers unbox it on video without being asked?
